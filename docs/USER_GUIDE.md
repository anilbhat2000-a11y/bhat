# User guide

For employees and managers of 3C IT Solutions and Telecoms India Ltd.

## Signing in

Open the LMS address your L&D team gave you (for example `https://lms.3cit.local`) on the office network or the VPN. Sign in with your work email and password.

- After 5 wrong passwords, sign-in is paused for 15 minutes.
- You are signed out after 30 minutes without activity.
- Change your password under **My profile → Change password**. If you forget it, ask an L&D admin to reset it.

The LMS works on desktop, tablet and phone browsers.

## For employees

### Your home page

**Your learning** shows, from top to bottom:

- **Announcements** from L&D.
- **Remedial training**, if you failed an assessment (see below).
- **Upcoming live sessions** you registered for.
- **Assigned courses** with their status and a countdown to the due date.
- **Assessments to do**, with a countdown to each due date.
- **My progress by subject**, with a progress bar for each subject and level you have started.
- **Recommended for you**: courses in subjects where your scores are below the pass mark.
- **My certificates**, with download and verification links.

Use the search box to find courses and lessons.

### Taking a course

1. Open a course from **Assigned courses** or the **Course catalogue**. The catalogue lists every published course by subject and level (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced). You can start any of them yourself.
2. Courses are split into modules, each with an estimated time. Complete lessons in order:
   - **Text and PDF:** read, then press **Mark complete**.
   - **Video:** playback resumes where you stopped. Captions are available. Some courses require you to watch most of each video (for example 90%) before you can mark it complete; skipping ahead does not count.
   - **Hands-on lab:** tick each step as you do it; ticking the last step completes the lesson.
   - **Live session:** register, join with the meeting link at the time shown (in your local time). The lesson completes when the trainer records your attendance.
3. When you come back, the course shows where you stopped.
4. Some courses are complete only after you pass their assessment, not just their lessons.
5. When every lesson is done, the **Completion check** at the bottom of the course page unlocks. Press **Take the completion check**; passing it earns the course certificate.
6. When you finish, answer the two-question **Course feedback** at the bottom of the course page.
7. Ask questions in **Questions and answers** at the bottom of the course page. Colleagues and L&D can answer.

### Assessments

Open **All my assessments** to see what is open, due, or overdue.

- **Start** opens an attempt. Questions and answer options are shuffled for each attempt, and each attempt may draw different questions.
- A **timer** shows the time left. When it reaches zero, your answers are submitted automatically.
- Question types: single choice, multiple choice ("select all that apply"), true/false, fill in the blank (type the word), matching (choose the right item for each line), and short answer (written, marked by a reviewer). Some questions start with a **scenario** to read first.
- **Proctored assessments** (usually the annual exam) start in full screen. Leaving full screen or switching to another tab or window counts as a violation. After the allowed number, your answers are submitted as they stand. Close other programs before you start.
- After you submit, you see your score and whether you passed. If the assessment allows it, you also see the correct answers and explanations.
- If the assessment has short answers, your score appears after a reviewer marks them. The reviewer's notes are shown with the result.
- You have a limited number of attempts per period (usually 3).

### If you do not pass

The LMS sets up **remedial training**, shown on your home page:

1. Work through the linked course again (your video positions and lab ticks are kept).
2. Your retake opens after a waiting time, usually 24 hours.

When both are done, start the retake from **All my assessments**. Your manager may add a comment to the plan.

### Certificates

Passing an assessment gives you a certificate for that subject and level, usually valid for 12 months. Download the PDF from your home page or **My certifications**. Each certificate has a verification code and a QR code; anyone can scan it or open the verification link to confirm it is genuine. Before it expires, pass the assessment again to renew it.

### Your profile

**My profile** holds your contact details, self-rated skills, education, work experience, external certifications, and languages. Your manager can see your profile. Department, job title, manager and photo are set by L&D.

## For managers

Open the **Manager portal** (`/manager`). You see everyone in your reporting line, directly and indirectly, and no one else.

- **My team** lists each person with their progress. Click a name for the full record:
  - assigned courses and their status, and a form to **make a course mandatory** for that person with a due date;
  - **weak topics**: subjects where their average score is below the pass mark;
  - **remedial plans**, where you can approve or add a comment;
  - course progress, every assessment attempt (with score, and proctoring flags if any), certificates, and overdue assessments;
  - their profile.
- **Team summary** shows a **subject heat map** for your team (certified, weak, in progress, not started), course completion per person, and certifications by subject. **Download team CSV** for Excel, or **Print or save as PDF**.
- **Team sessions** lists your team's live sessions; you can record attendance.
- Every Monday you get an email listing team members with overdue assessments.

Managers can also take courses and assessments in the learner portal (`/learn`).

## Accessibility

All pages work with a keyboard, have visible focus, labelled form fields and good colour contrast. Videos have captions. Heat-map cells show their meaning as text, not only colour. If something is hard to use, tell your L&D team.
